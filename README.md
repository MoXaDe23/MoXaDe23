## 👋 About Me

Hi there! I'm **Nelson Mugume**, a medical doctor from Uganda 🇺🇬 passionate about the intersection of **medicine, data science, artificial intelligence, software systems, and innovation**.

My journey started with diagnosing and treating patients, solving clinical problems, and making decisions from incomplete information. That same problem-solving mindset eventually led me into **data analytics, machine learning, AI, and software development**.

Today, I enjoy building solutions that turn **raw data, complex workflows, and real-world problems into useful systems, insights, and decision-support tools**.

My interests increasingly extend beyond healthcare into areas such as:

- 🏥 Health technology & clinical decision support
- 🤖 Artificial intelligence & machine learning
- 📊 Data analytics & business intelligence
- 🧠 NLP and intelligent information systems
- 💻 AI-assisted software development
- 🌍 Public health & population analytics
- 🌊 Environmental & hydrological modelling
- 🌱 Agriculture, forestry & enterprise systems
- 📱 Practical digital tools for African contexts

> **I don't just want to analyze data — I want to build systems that help people make better decisions.**

---

## 🧠 My Mission

To harness **medicine, data, AI, and technology** to build practical solutions that improve healthcare, strengthen decision-making, automate difficult workflows, and make useful technology more accessible.

I am particularly interested in problems where multiple disciplines meet:

**Medicine + Data + AI + Systems Thinking + Real-World Implementation**

I am always open to collaborating on meaningful **data science, AI, health-tech, analytics, research, and software projects**.

- 🤖 Building practical **AI-powered applications**

# 🚀 Selected Projects & Areas of Work

### 🏥 HealthMate AI
A multilingual AI-powered health assistant exploring **symptom triage, medical knowledge support, and accessible digital healthcare**, with future potential for low-bandwidth environments.

**Focus:** Healthcare AI • Machine Learning • Streamlit • Clinical Decision Support

---

### 💳 OutlaysEA
An AI-assisted expense management application designed around **receipt scanning, OCR, automated transaction capture, analytics, budgeting, and offline-first workflows**.

**Focus:** AI-assisted development • OCR • Data architecture • PWA • Analytics

---

### 🌊 Lake Victoria Hydrological Modelling
Data-driven modelling and optimization of **Lake Victoria water levels, inflows, outflows, rainfall, evaporation, and dam operating strategies**.

**Focus:** Python • Time Series • Optimization • Hydrology • Data Visualization

---

### 🩺 Medical Evaluation & Readiness Analytics
Structured digital workflows for capturing and analysing **medical evaluation data, clinical findings, follow-up requirements, fitness assessments, and operational readiness indicators**.

**Focus:** Healthcare Analytics • Excel/Sheets • Automation • Data Quality • Dashboards

---

### 🌲 Forestry & Agribusiness Systems
Exploring digital systems that connect **forest operations, logistics, processing, inventory, analytics, finance, and enterprise decision-making**.

**Focus:** Systems Analysis • Databases • Business Intelligence • Workflow Automation
